How much does a divorce cost in Oklahoma?

A cheap, uncontested Oklahoma divorce through us totals about $412 without children, court fee included. Here's where that number comes from and how it compares to your other options.

Your total cost with Express Divorce Services

Every Oklahoma divorce has two costs: the fee for preparing your documents, and the filing fee you pay to the court. Here's what you'll pay in total.

  • Without minor children: $412.08 total. $159.95 to us ($150 service + $9.95 online processing) plus the $252.13 court filing fee.
  • Spouse cannot be located: $462.08 total. $209.95 to us plus the $252.13 court filing fee.
  • With minor children: $512.08 total. $259.95 to us plus the $252.13 court filing fee. Add $50 if you'd like to request a waiver of the 90-day waiting period.

What can make a divorce cost more?

  • Disagreements. If you and your spouse can't agree on property, debts, or custody, the divorce becomes contested, and costs rise quickly. Keeping it uncontested is the single biggest way to keep it cheap.
  • A spouse who can't be found. Notice has to be published, which takes additional work.
  • Minor children. Custody, visitation, and child support paperwork add documents, and Oklahoma's 90-day waiting period applies unless the court waives it.

How to keep your Oklahoma divorce affordable

  • Agree with your spouse on all of the terms before you file.
  • Gather your information ahead of time: names, addresses, dates of marriage and separation, and details about children, property, and debts.
  • If you have children, use the Oklahoma child support calculator to estimate support before you finalize your agreement.
